Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about your cinema visit

Can I show the M-Ticket on my mobile phone at the cinema gate? ▼
Yes! All online bookings generate a digital M-Ticket with an encrypted QR code and booking reference. Simply present your phone screen to our gate usher for contactless scanning. No printouts required!
What are the ticket pricing tiers? ▼
We offer three distinct classes: VIP Pushback Recliner Lounge (₹350), Platinum Premium (₹220), and Gold Standard (₹150). All tiers include full Dolby Atmos audio immersion.
Is parking available at the complex? ▼
Yes! Parimalam Complex features 2-wheeler and 4-wheeler basement parking with 24/7 security surveillance and elevator access directly to our 2nd-floor cinema lobby.
Can I order food and snacks directly to my seat? ▼
Absolutely! You can add gourmet popcorn, loaded nachos, and chilled beverages during your online ticket booking or order via the concession counters during intermission.
